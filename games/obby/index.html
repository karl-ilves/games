❓ Obby Guide & Controls

🎮 Controls:

  • W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys: Movement
  • Space: Jump & Double Jump (press again in mid-air)
  • Shift: Sprint (faster run)
  • Mouse: Look around (drag) & zoom wheel
  • V: Toggle camera view (3rd Person / 1st Person)
  • R: Quick respawn to last checkpoint

🌟 Rules & Rewards:

  • Step on glowing Checkpoint Pads to save your spot and earn +5 Yards!
  • Dodge red lava, laser beams, and falling into the void.
  • Collect spinning Gold Coins along the track to buy hats, trails, and power boots!
  • Conquer all 10 stages (10/10) to earn +100 Yards and the golden trophy!

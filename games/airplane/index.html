How are you playing this game?

Tablet / Phone Controls

Use the on-screen buttons:

Up Arrow - pitches the plane up
Down Arrow - pitches the plane down
Left/Right Arrows - rolls the plane

Next to the arrows are the Throttle buttons:
+ speeds up, - slows down.

Computer Controls

Press W to increase speed, S to decrease speed.

Use A / D to steer left/right, and Arrow Up / Down to tilt nose.
